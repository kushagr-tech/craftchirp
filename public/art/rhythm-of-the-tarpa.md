## Karigari Ki Kahani • The Provenance Story
The Tarpa dance is the philosophical heart of Warli tribal culture. Men and women entwine hands and move in an infinite spiral around the Tarpa player—a wind instrument crafted from dried gourd and bamboo. The spiral mirrors the movement of the sun, the cyclical nature of seasons, and the collective heartbeat of the village after the winter harvest.
