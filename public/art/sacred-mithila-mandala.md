## Karigari Ki Kahani • The Provenance Story
Rooted in the ancient Madhubani tradition of northern Bihar, this mandala represents cosmic harmony and fertility. Using the Kachni (fine hatched line) and Bharni (solid pigment fill) techniques, concentric rings of sacred lotus blossoms, swimming fish, and solar rays radiate from a central sacred point.
