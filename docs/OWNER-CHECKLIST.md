## 1. Indian Export & Banking Registrations

To receive international foreign currency payments in INR to an Indian bank account and export goods legally, the following statutory registrations are required:

- [ ] **1. Importer-Exporter Code (IEC):**
  - **What it is:** A 10-digit identification code issued by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT), Ministry of Commerce & Industry, Government of India.
  - **How to obtain:** Apply online at [dgft.gov.in](https://www.dgft.gov.in) with your PAN, cancelled bank cheque, and Aadhaar/business proof.
  - **Timeframe:** 1–2 business days.

- [ ] **2. GST LUT (Letter of Undertaking) for Zero-Rated Exports:**
  - **What it is:** Allows you to export goods without paying upfront Integrated GST (IGST) under Rule 96A of CGST Rules.
  - **How to obtain:** File Form GST RFD-11 annually on the GST Portal (`gst.gov.in`) before exporting.
  - **Why essential:** Ensures your art sales to foreign collectors are 0% GST rated, avoiding working capital lockup.

- [ ] **3. Authorized Dealer (AD) Code Registration:**
  - **What it is:** A 14-digit code issued by your bank (where you receive export proceeds) and registered with Indian Customs at your export port/air cargo hub (e.g., Mumbai Air Cargo Complex / Sahar).
  - **Requirement:** Ask your bank's foreign exchange/trade desk for an AD Code Letter on bank letterhead, then have DHL/FedEx register it in the Indian Customs EDI system (ICEGATE).

- [ ] **4. e-FIRA / e-FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate):**
  - **What it is:** Legal proof issued by your payment gateway (Razorpay International / Stripe India / PayPal) and partner bank confirming foreign inward remittance.
  - **Mandatory RBI Purpose Codes:**
    - `P0102` / `S0102`: Export of physical merchandise/goods (Original Art & Handicrafts).
  - **Action:** Retain all monthly e-FIRA downloads for reconciliation with your Chartered Accountant (CA) for EDPMS (Export Data Processing and Monitoring System) closure.

## 2. Customs HS Tariff Codes & Courier Coordination

- [ ] **1. Primary HS Tariff Codes:**
  - **Original Folk Paintings on MDF:**
    - **HS Code:** `9701.21.00` (*Paintings, drawings and pastels, executed entirely by hand*)
    - **US Duty:** 0% (UNESCO Florence Agreement)
    - **EU Duty:** Exempt under standard tariff or €3.00 flat interim duty under EU 2026/382 for small consignments.
    - **Commercial Invoice Description:** *"Original 1-of-1 Folk Art Painting executed entirely by hand on moisture-sealed MDF with purified natural mud and mineral pigments by artist Rachana Agarwal."*
  - **Handmade Terracotta Jewellery:**
    - **HS Code:** `7117.90.90` (*Other imitation jewellery — ceramic/terracotta with surgical steel findings*)
    - **US Duty:** ~5% standard MFN tariff
    - **EU Duty:** Standard EU ceramic/imitation jewellery rate (~4%).

- [ ] **2. DHL Express & FedEx Account Setup:**
  - Open a corporate export account with **DHL Express** or **FedEx Cross-Border Services**.
  - Request their **Paperless Trade (PLT) / Electronic Trade Documents (ETD)** feature so commercial invoices and shipping labels are transmitted digitally to foreign customs prior to aircraft arrival.
  - Inform your carrier account manager that all international packages will be shipped with **Full Declared Value Cargo Insurance**.

## 3. Organic Cow-Dung Base & Quarantine Compliance

Because Craftchirp’s unique heritage signature is the natural cow-dung mud plaster foundation, international agricultural inspection services (such as USDA-APHIS in the US and EFSA in Europe) require clarity that the material poses zero biological risk:

- [ ] **1. Standard Artisan Material Declaration Letter:**
  - Print and enclose one signed copy of the **Craftchirp Studio Material Declaration** inside the packing slip pouch for every international shipment:
  - **Required Language:**
    > *"I, Rachana Agarwal, creator and founder of Craftchirp, hereby declare that the accompanying hand-painted artwork incorporates a dried, non-infectious, mineralized substrate composed of triple-strained Indian cow-dung and riverbed clay ground onto engineered high-density MDF.*
    > *The organic mixture has undergone a high-temperature sun-curing and thermal drying process exceeding 7 days, eliminating all microbial activity, viable organisms, pathogens, moisture, and odors.*
    > *The surface is sealed with an organic beeswax and acacia gum binder, rendering the material non-viable, chemically inert, and solely intended as a fine art cultural artifact. It is free of soil organisms, plant seeds, and infectious matter."*

- [ ] **2. Phytosanitary / Veterinary Status:**
  - Processed, fully cured decorative folk artifacts with mineralized animal dung grounds are classified internationally as *fully processed cultural craft items* and are exempt from live veterinary import permits provided the declaration letter is attached.

## 4. Packing & Crating Standards for Rigid MDF

- [ ] **1. Acid-Free Glassine Barrier:** Wrap the face of the painting in archival acid-free glassine paper. Never use plastic wrap directly against the relief lines.
- [ ] **2. Corner Shock Protectors:** Affix 35mm high-density polyethylene U-profiles or cardboard edge protectors to all 4 corners of the MDF board.
- [ ] **3. Waterproof Cushioning:** Wrap in 2 layers of heavy-duty bubble wrap and seal in a 50-micron polyethylene moisture barrier bag.
- [ ] **4. 5-Ply Honeycomb Armor Carton / Timber Crate:**
  - For paintings under 50 cm: Use double-wall 5-ply honeycomb structural boxes (min 80 kg puncture strength).
  - For paintings over 60 cm: Reinforce the box with rigid MDF perimeter struts or thin plywood crating.
- [ ] **5. Certificate of Authenticity Placement:** Place the signed Certificate of Authenticity and Material Declaration letter in an archival glassine sleeve taped to the reverse of the inner board.

## 5. Real Product Photography Shot List (Zero-AI Policy)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Launch Blocker:** Current catalogue images are development placeholders. No artwork can be listed as available for sale with AI-generated visuals. Every piece requires 5 genuine high-resolution photographs taken in natural daylight.

For each painting added to the catalogue, capture the following 5 essential photographs:

1. **Shot 1: Full Frontal Showcase (Hero Shot)**
   - **Setup:** Mount painting on an easel or neutral off-white wall. Camera perfectly parallel to the canvas (zero keystoning or perspective distortion).
   - **Lighting:** Soft, indirect morning or late afternoon daylight. No harsh direct flash or yellow incandescent lamps.
   - **Purpose:** Primary catalog card and zoom baseline.

2. **Shot 2: 45° Raking Light Texture Relief**
   - **Setup:** Angle the light source from one side at 45 degrees to cast subtle micro-shadows across the raised rice paste brushstrokes and mud grain.
   - **Purpose:** Proves physical relief texture and hand-applied mud foundation to collectors inspecting the piece.

3. **Shot 3: Macro Detail (100% Crop / Close-Up)**
   - **Setup:** Macro lens or high-resolution close-up focused on a 5 cm section of intricate Warli figures or Madhubani line work.
   - **Purpose:** Demonstrates fine bamboo-reed brushwork, authentic pigment variation, and absence of mechanical dot patterns.

4. **Shot 4: In-Room Scale & Human Perspective**
   - **Setup:** Display the artwork mounted on a wall above a wooden sideboard, credenza, or held by Rachana in her studio.
   - **Purpose:** Gives buyers an immediate intuitive sense of physical scale, framing presence, and interior harmony.

5. **Shot 5: Reverse Side & In-Progress / Packaging**
   - **Setup:** Photo of the back of the MDF board showing pre-installed brass hanging hardware, hand-signed serial number, and custom shock-proof crating.
   - **Purpose:** Solidifies collector trust regarding structural durability and ready-to-hang museum standard.
