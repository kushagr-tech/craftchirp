## Step 1: Photographing Your Artwork (Natural Light & 5 Angles)

Never use artificial phone filters or AI image enhancers. Collectors fall in love with the real texture of your cow-dung base and the relief strokes of your rice paste.

### The 5 Essential Photographs to Take:

1. **Shot 1: Full Front View (The Hero)**
   - **Where:** Mount the painting on an easel or neutral off-white wall.
   - **Light:** Bright, indirect morning sunlight (no harsh direct glare or shadows).
   - **Camera:** Hold your phone or camera straight and parallel to the artwork so all four corners are square.

2. **Shot 2: Side Angle Texture Relief (45° Raking Light)**
   - **Where:** Position the light source from one side at a 45° angle.
   - **Why:** This casts tiny micro-shadows along the raised mud texture and bamboo-reed strokes, proving to the collector that this is an authentic textured relief, not a flat print.

3. **Shot 3: Macro Close-Up (Intricate Detail)**
   - **Where:** Move your lens within 10 to 15 cm of the most intricate Warli figures or Madhubani line work.
   - **Why:** Shows the exquisite human handcraft and natural pigment grain.

4. **Shot 4: In-Room Scale (Living Sanctuary)**
   - **Where:** Lean or mount the painting above a wooden credenza, desk, or hold it gently in your studio.
   - **Why:** Helps the buyer understand the real physical size and picture it in their home.

5. **Shot 5: Reverse Board & Hardware**
   - **Where:** Photograph the back of the MDF board showing the pre-installed brass D-rings and hand-written signature.

## Step 2: Open Your Craftchirp Admin Portal

1. On your computer or tablet, open your browser (Chrome or Safari).
2. Go to: **`https://craftchirp.com/admin`**
3. Log in with your secure studio credentials.
4. You will see two main tabs:
   - **Catalog Inventory:** Where all your artworks are listed.
   - **Orders & Fulfillment:** Where new sales appear.

---

## Step 3: Add a New Artwork & Upload Images

1. Click the terracotta button labeled **"+ Add New Original Work"**.
2. Drag and drop your 5 photographs into the upload box.
3. The system automatically creates fast, modern image formats (WebP/AVIF) and sharp zooms.
4. Select which photograph is your **Primary (Hero) Image** (Shot 1).

---

## Step 4: Write Title & "Karigari Ki Kahani" (The Story)

Folk art is storytelling. Collectors buy because they feel connected to your 15+ years of practice and the spiritual meaning behind the piece.

1. **Artwork Title:** Choose an evocative name (e.g., *"Tarpa Dance Celebration"*, *"Tree of Life with Solar Motifs"*).
2. **Folk Style:** Select **Warli** or **Madhubani** from the dropdown.
3. **The Story:** Write 2–3 paragraphs in your own voice answering:
   - What village or ceremonial ritual inspired this piece?
   - What do the symbols represent (e.g., the circular spiral of the Tarpa dance representing the continuous cycle of life)?
   - How was the cow-dung ground prepared for this specific board?

---

## Step 5: Enter Dimensions, Weight & HS Tariff Code

1. **Dimensions:**
   - Measure with a tape: Width, Height, and Board Thickness in centimeters (cm).
   - *The system will automatically convert these into inches for American collectors.*
2. **Weight:**
   - Weigh the finished painting on a digital kitchen or parcel scale in grams (e.g., `1850` g).
   - Enter estimated packaged weight (typically artwork weight + 750 g for crating).
3. **International HS Tariff Code:**
   - For paintings, choose: **`9701.21.00`** *(Original paintings executed entirely by hand)*.
   - For jewellery, choose: **`7117.90.90`** *(Handmade imitation jewellery)*.
   - *This ensures US customs charges 0% duty and EU customs applies the correct craft category.*

---

## Step 6: Set Fixed Prices Per Currency

Craftchirp uses **fixed pricing per currency**, not fluctuating live bank exchange rates. This gives collectors stability and ensures you receive the exact compensation you intended.

1. Enter your desired price in each currency:
   - **USD ($):** e.g., `$420.00`
   - **EUR (€):** e.g., `€385.00`
   - **GBP (£):** e.g., `£340.00`
   - **INR (₹):** e.g., `₹34,500`
2. Framing status: Select **Unframed (brass hanging hardware installed)** or **Custom Framed**.

## Step 7: Publish to the Gallery

1. Review your preview card on the screen.
2. Click **"Publish Artwork"**.
3. **Done!** The artwork is immediately live in the `/gallery`, discoverable by Google and AI search assistants, and eligible for the "See It On Your Wall" room visualizer.

*(Need to make changes later? Simply click "Edit" on the artwork card in your Admin portal at any time.)*

## Step 8: What Happens When an Artwork Sells?

1. **Automatic Reservation:** When a collector enters checkout, the piece is reserved for 15 minutes.
2. **Instant Payment & Sale Notification:**
   - As soon as the customer's payment clears, the artwork status automatically switches from **Available** to **SOLD**.
   - No one else can purchase it.
   - You receive an email notification: *"New Order #CC-2026-XXXX from [Collector Name] in [City, Country]"*.
3. Go to `/admin` → **Orders & Fulfillment** to view the buyer’s full shipping address and phone number.

## Step 9: Print Certificate of Authenticity & Pack to Museum Spec

### Printing the Certificate:
1. In your Admin Order view, click **"Print Certificate of Authenticity"**.
2. Feed a sheet of archival off-white 250–300 GSM cotton paper into your color printer.
3. Sign the certificate in the wet ink signature box and stamp your artist seal.
4. Place the certificate and the **Artisan Material Declaration Letter** inside a waterproof glassine envelope.

### Packing Your MDF Artwork (The 4-Layer Spec):
- **Layer 1 (The Face):** Wrap the front of the painting in acid-free glassine paper. Tightly tape only on the back of the MDF. *(Never use standard plastic wrap directly against the mud!)*
- **Layer 2 (Corners):** Snap the 4 rigid high-density corner foam protectors onto all four corners of the board.
- **Layer 3 (Moisture Barrier):** Wrap the board in 2 full layers of heavy bubble wrap and slide into the waterproof seal bag.
- **Layer 4 (The Armor Box):** Place inside the 5-ply heavy honeycomb structural box. Fill any empty spaces with crumpled kraft paper so the board cannot shift when gently shaken.

---

## Step 10: Book Express Courier & Enter Tracking

1. **Book Courier:**
   - Open your **DHL Express** or **FedEx** corporate portal.
   - Paste the buyer's shipping address and mandatory phone number.
   - Enter package dimensions and weight.
   - Set Customs Declaration: *"HS Code 9701.21.00 — Original hand-painted Warli folk artwork on MDF by Rachana Agarwal"*.
   - Check **Full Cargo Insurance** box.
   - Generate and print the Air Waybill (AWB) and 3 copies of the commercial invoice.
2. **Affix Labels:** Stick the courier pouch firmly to the front of the crate.
3. **Enter Tracking in Admin:**
   - Return to `https://craftchirp.com/admin` → **Orders**.
   - Click on the order, select carrier (e.g. `DHL Express`), paste the tracking number (e.g. `1234567890`), and click **"Mark Order Dispatched"**.
   - The system automatically sends an email to the collector with their live tracking link and congratulations!

## Frequently Asked Questions for Rachana

**Q: Can I put an artwork on temporary hold for someone who contacted me on Instagram?**  
**A:** Yes! In `/admin`, find the artwork and click the toggle from "Available" to "Reserved". When they are ready to purchase, either switch it back to "Available" and send them the link, or create a private acquisition invoice.

**Q: What if someone from Russia wants to acquire a piece?**  
**A:** Because automated card payments to Russia are paused due to international banking sanctions, Russian visitors will see an inquiry form at `/ru/inquiry`. When an inquiry arrives, it goes to your email. You can coordinate bespoke acquisition details directly.

**Q: Where can I see my export remittance certificates (e-FIRA)?**  
**A:** At the end of every calendar month, log into your Razorpay International or Stripe India portal under **"Reports" → "Remittance / e-FIRA"**. Download the PDF certificates and share them with your Chartered Accountant for GST LUT closure.
